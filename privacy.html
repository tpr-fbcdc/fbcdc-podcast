Privacy Policy

FBCDC Sermon Uploader is an internal tool of The First Baptist Church of the City of Washington, D.C. Church staff use it to upload sermon videos to the church's own YouTube channel.

What it accesses

The tool signs in only to the church's own YouTube account. It uploads sermon videos and sets their titles, descriptions, thumbnails and playlist. It does not read, collect or store information about anyone else.

What it stores

The sign-in token is kept in the macOS Keychain on the church's computer. It is never shared with anyone or sent anywhere except Google's own servers.

The public

Members of the public do not use this tool, and it collects no information from people who watch or listen to our sermons. YouTube's own privacy policy covers your use of YouTube.
